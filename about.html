ABOUT BUDA

Passion meets precision.

BUDA Auto Styling is a local automotive styling concept created around the culture of clean builds, proper fitment and quality workmanship.

OUR STORY

Made for people who care about cars.

BUDA Auto Styling focuses on the parts of car culture that transform a vehicle: suspension, wheels, fitment and presentation.

Our approach is to help customers understand their options before choosing a setup, while providing a professional experience from enquiry to installation.

MISSION

Quality builds, done with purpose.

To provide quality automotive styling, suspension and car-care solutions while helping enthusiasts create safe, personalised vehicles.

VISION

A trusted home for enthusiasts.

To become a trusted local destination for people looking for quality products, professional workmanship and excellent service.

Suspension. Wheels. Stance. Care.
