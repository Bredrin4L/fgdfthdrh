What information should I include in an enquiry?

Include your vehicle make, model, year, the service you are interested in and any parts you already have. This helps the team understand your plans.

Can I choose a very low suspension setup?

Setup choices should consider road conditions, tyre clearance, suspension travel and local roadworthiness requirements. A suitable setup should remain practical and safe to drive.

Do product prices appear on this website?

This student project presents product categories rather than live stock or prices. Availability and pricing would need to be confirmed directly with the business.
